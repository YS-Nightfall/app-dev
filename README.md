# My Favorite Movie: Independence Day

## About
**Independence Day** (1996) is a sci-fi action film directed by **Roland Emmerich**. Massive alien ships appear over cities around the world, and humanity has to unite to fight back.

## Why I Like It
- Exciting action and big *blockbuster* moments
- Great mix of humor and suspense
- A cast that works well together
- Iconic scenes, like the ships hovering over major cities

## Main Characters
1. Captain Steven Hiller (Will Smith)
2. David Levinson (Jeff Goldblum)
3. President Thomas Whitmore (Bill Pullman)

## Favorite Quote
